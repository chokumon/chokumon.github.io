Day 9　TOEIC Part 7 練習問題

相手の希望を書き出し、返事の条件に1つずつ当てる

レベルの目安 500〜850 点

使い方
  1. 下の説明にある時間を目安に10問を解く（答えはメモ）
  2. 問題の下の「答えと解説を見る」で答え合わせ。空欄でどんな「問い」を立てるかを読む
  3. まちがえた問題は、英文を前から声に出して読む

今日は Part 7 だけです。テキストメッセージ（2問）→ 記事（3問）→ 問い合わせと返信の2通のメール（5問）。全部で12分はあくまで目安です。まず自分が何分かかったかを記録しましょう。意図問題は、引用の直前の発言と直後の反応で決めます。最後のセットは、1通目の希望（数・色・届く日）を書き出し、2通目の値段の表と条件（できる日・送り方）に1つずつ当てます。日付は、実際に数えて確かめましょう。

Article

Kowhai Hardware Marks 50 Years with New Rental Service

WELLINGTON (14 March) — Kowhai Hardware, the family-owned chain with four shops in the Wellington region, will celebrate its 50th anniversary next month by launching a tool rental service.

From 1 April, customers will be able to rent more than 200 tools, from ladders to floor sanders, for periods of one day to two weeks. Rental prices will start at NZ$12 a day.

"Many of our customers only need a particular tool once or twice," said managing director Mere Tainui, whose grandparents opened the first Kowhai shop on Cuba Street in 1975. "Renting is cheaper for them, and it means fewer tools sitting unused in garages."

The service will begin at the Cuba Street and Petone shops, which have the most storage space. If demand is strong, it will be extended to the company's other two locations later in the year.

To mark the anniversary, all four shops will also hold a customer appreciation day on Saturday, 5 April, with a free barbecue lunch and workshops on basic home repairs.
